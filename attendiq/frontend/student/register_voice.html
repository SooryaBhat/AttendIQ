🎤 Recording guidelines

  • Record at least 5–10 seconds of natural speech
  • Speak clearly and at a normal conversational pace
  • Record in a quiet room with minimal background noise
  • Hold the microphone 10–15 cm from your mouth
  • Read a sentence aloud — avoid humming or silence
  • WAV format gives the best recognition accuracy
